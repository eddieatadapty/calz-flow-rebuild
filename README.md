# Calz Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/calz-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Calz users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Calz's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Put their goal on the paywall | +15–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Let them scan a meal before they pay | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Proof where the decision happens | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Make the yearly saving impossible to miss | +10–15% ARPU, from a bigger yearly share |
| 05 | Give the gift to the people who say no | +10–15% ARPU, from people who would have left |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Calz's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Calz. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
